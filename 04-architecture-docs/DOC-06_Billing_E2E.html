Invariants (apply to every section below) ① Double-entry: every money movement posts balanced ledger lines (BL-15); no screen writes balances directly. ② Issued statutory documents are immutable — corrections are new documents (credit note, new invoice), never edits (§3). ③ Every money/legal action requires step-up identity AU-09; irreversible actions (refund payout, write-off, period lock, series edit) are four-eyes. ④ All approvals are async (BL-08 queue + notification), never blocking modals — the DS-03 SLA chip pattern shows queue age everywhere. ⑤ Every action emits a DOC-05 BILL-03xx audit event with before/after hashes. ⑥ Billing documents retained 8 years (GV-06); legal holds (GV-07) override.

ReceiptBL-05statutory · immutableOne receipt per successful tender leg. Reprint allowed (watermarked DUPLICATE, AUTH-0109 audit); never re-issue.
RefundBL-06approval-gatedMoney back to patient. Requires linked receipt(s); refund mode follows original tender where possible (UPI→UPI reversal, card→card void/refund, cash→cash from open drawer session). Payout only after BL-08 approval (§5).
Credit noteBL-07statutory · immutableFiscal negation (full/partial) of an issued invoice. Mandatory before/with any refund of an invoiced amount and for post-issue corrections. References invoice number; GST treatment §6.4.
LedgerBL-15derived, append-onlyDouble-entry journal; patient, payer, cash/bank, GST-liability control accounts. Screens read balances from here — single source of financial truth.
Day bookBL-18derivedAll documents + tenders per branch-day; must tie to BL-09 cash session totals before EOD close (§9).
GST exportBL-19regulatoryPeriod file (GSTR-1 format: exempt outward supplies + taxable exceptions + credit notes). Export hash audited (BILL-0305); generation allowed only after all branch-days in period are EOD-closed.

Scope
Series are per branch (KOR = Koramangala) per financial year (26-27), matching GST registration (AD-09 registrations). Multi-GSTIN chains get isolated series per GSTIN.
Offline branches
Edge mode (IN-10) may collect against an already-issued invoice but may not issue INV/CRN numbers offline — gapless allocation is server-only. FO offline fallback: take advance (ADV from a reserved offline sub-series ADV/KOR-OFF/…), convert on sync.
Series edits
Changing prefix/format mid-year is blocked; new series start only at FY boundary or new-branch creation. Emergency change = platform-operator action (PL) + ADMN-0503 four-eyes.

5 · GST rules

TopicRuleScreens
Default: exempt healthcareDiagnostic and healthcare services by a clinical establishment are GST-exempt (Notification 12/2017, entry 74). Default document type is Bill of Supply / exempt tax invoice: lines carry SAC 9993 + EXEMPT label, zero tax, and the mandated exemption remark printed in footer. No ITC implications surface to FO.BL-02, BL-10 (catalog flag)
Taxable exceptionsCatalog items flagged TAXABLE in BL-10: film/CD/print media copies of studies, courier of reports, non-clinical merchandise, cosmetic/wellness packages not qualifying as healthcare, canteen items at branch, and B2B services to non-establishment clients (e.g., telerad reads sold to another entity, RD-02 context). Mixed invoice = single document, per-line tax treatment; taxable lines force GSTIN block + HSN/SAC print.BL-10, BL-02
CGST/SGST vs IGSTPlace of supply vs branch GSTIN state: same state ⇒ CGST+SGST split; different state ⇒ IGST. For walk-in B2C services place-of-supply is branch state ⇒ effectively always CGST/SGST. IGST paths arise on B2B inter-state (corporate payer with out-of-state GSTIN on BL-11, telerad B2B). Engine input: branch GSTIN (AD-09), payer GSTIN + state (BL-11), item taxability (BL-10), rate table (BL-13).BL-13, BL-11, AD-09
Credit notes & filingCRN within the same open period nets in BL-19 export. CRN against an invoice from an already-filed period is allowed (until the statutory cut-off: 30 Nov following FY / annual return, enforced by BL-13 config) but is flagged POST-FILING, requires finance-controller 4-eyes+ (§4), and is exported in the current period's GSTR-1 CDNR section referencing the original invoice date/number. After statutory cut-off: CRN blocked; remedy is write-off (BL-17) or goodwill refund without fiscal negation, per finance policy.BL-07, BL-19, BL-13
Period lockBL-13 period lock (per GSTIN, per month) after BL-19 export is generated and marked "filed". Locked period ⇒ no document may be issued with a date in it (all issuance is server-timestamped anyway), no CRN dated into it, day books frozen. Unlock = platform-operator + finance controller 4-eyes+, fully audited (ADMN-0503 + SEC-0604 on attempts).BL-13, BL-19, BL-18
Export fileBL-19 produces GSTR-1-shaped JSON + CSV: B2C exempt summary, B2B taxable lines, CDNR credit notes, document-number ranges per series (gapless proof from AD-16). File hash logged BILL-0305; export download is AUTH-0109.BL-19

Entry pointPath

GR screens show the full financial trail read-only; grievance staff never mutate billing documents directly — they request via BL-08 like everyone else.
