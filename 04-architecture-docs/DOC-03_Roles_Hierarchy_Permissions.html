ScopeThis document defines the organisational hierarchy, the 13-role catalogue, the role × screen-group access matrix, ABAC conditions layered on top of RBAC, separation-of-duties (SoD) rules, and delegation / break-glass behaviour. Roles are provisioned in Keycloak (realm magnus-staff) via AD-03/AD-04; the authorization decision is made by the policy service (PDP) on every API call — the React frontend only reflects decisions (hide/disable), it never enforces them.

2. Role catalogue (13 roles)

Keycloak realm roles, prefixed mhms:. A user may hold multiple roles at multiple scope nodes (e.g. mhms:cashier @ BR-IND-01 + mhms:front-office @ BR-IND-02); the workspace chooser AU-10 resolves the active role+scope pair per session. Step-up = re-auth via AU-09 (per design contract: sign / money / legal actions).

4. ABAC conditions (applied after RBAC)

Policy service (Spring, PDP module) evaluates in order: RBAC group grant → ABAC conditions → obligations (mask, audit, step-up). Deny is default; every deny returns the masked-identity payload (#MRN-••••30) instead of a bare 403 when a patient record was the target (design contract, rendered per DS pattern).

5. Separation-of-duties rules

Enforced server-side by the policy service (not just UI): the approver principal must differ from the initiator principal, and where noted, from anyone in the initiator's delegation chain (DD-10). All four-eyes actions are irreversible-class per design contract.

RuleScreensConstraintEnforcement detail
Financial exception approval four-eyes BL-06, BL-07, BL-08, BL-12, BL-17 Initiator of a refund, credit note, above-slab discount, or write-off cannot approve it. Cashiers can never self-approve; a billing supervisor who initiates on BL-06/BL-17 must route to a different BSU or the branch manager in BL-08. approver_id ≠ initiator_id; both step-up AU-09; approval record immutable, visible BL-18 day book + AD-20
Access & role grants four-eyes AD-03, AD-04, AD-05 A branch/org admin cannot approve a role request they raised, and can never approve any change to their own account (including unlock AU-14 and MFA reset AU-15). Org-admin role grants require a second org admin or the tenant owner. Self-target check on subject_user_id; queue AD-05 hides own items from approver view
Export governance four-eyes + DPO GV-15, BL-19, AD-12 Bulk export requester (any role) cannot approve; approval requires DPO (or privacy officer for < 500 records). DPO's own export requests route to org admin + platform-op co-sign. Export job blocked in state PENDING_APPROVAL; watermarked output; logged GV-15 + AD-20
Operator support access cross-org four-eyes PL-12 Platform operator requesting tenant data access cannot approve it — approval must come from the tenant's org admin (and DPO is notified). A second platform operator confirms activation (four-eyes inside NUMINACORE) before the grant goes live. Two approvals (tenant + operator peer); grant ≤ 8h; auto-review entry GV-03
Patient merge four-eyes FO-08 FO user queues merge candidates; only branch manager resolves, and not for candidates they themselves queued. Irreversible-class; pre-merge snapshot retained per retention policy (10y radiology)
Regulatory corrections IR-04, TK-10 Form F corrections (IR-04) authored by a radiologist require branch-manager approval; dose registry corrections (TK-10) by a technologist require radiologist or branch-admin countersign. Original record never overwritten — correction appended, both visible in IR-01 register
Clinical sign integrity RD-09, RD-12, CS-04 Not four-eyes, but exclusive: only the authoring radiologist signs their report (RD-09); addenda (RD-12) may be by another radiologist but never silently replace. CS-04 override must be a radiologist who is not the performing technologist's supervisor-of-record for that shift (prevents pressure override). Signature binds report hash (see DOC-04 L7); overrides feed GV-03 review
