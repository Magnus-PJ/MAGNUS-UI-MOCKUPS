GS-01 Global omni-search & command palette

MVP1-CORE · ADDITION
G18 Global Search — every workspace, Ctrl/⌘ K
Baseline: NET-NEW · DOC-14 UX-1 (closes the 8-of-232 search gap)
Purpose & workflow: One box, opened with Ctrl/⌘ K from anywhere, that finds everything the signed-in role is allowed to touch — patients (masked until a purpose is picked), orders/accessions, invoices/receipts, reports, screens and ACTIONS — and executes commands ("collect payment", "issue token") in ≤2 keystroke-steps. Every query and reveal is purpose-logged. This is the single biggest click-saver in the product.

GS-02 My tasks — unified inbox

MVP1-CORE · ADDITION
G18 Global Search & Tasks — one template, role-parameterised
Baseline: NET-NEW · DOC-14 UX-4 (athenaOne-class task-first navigation)
Purpose & workflow: One place that answers “what needs ME right now” — every queue that pages this user, aggregated with counts, SLA chips and deep links. Role-parameterised: a radiologist sees criticals awaiting send/ack, overdue reads, consults; a cashier sees approvals, pending confirmations, EOD tasks. Home screens keep their dashboards; this is the action feed.
