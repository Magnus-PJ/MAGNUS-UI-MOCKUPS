Auto-downgrade rule radiologist confirm always required
1 · Detect
System flags a STAT order when justification is blank, matches an administrative-reason lexicon, or the orderer is under a governance restriction.
2 · Radiologist confirm
The flagged order is queued to the duty radiologist with the justification text and clinical context. No priority is ever lowered automatically. The radiologist either confirms STAT or downgrades to Urgent/Routine with a reason code, signed under their own identity.
3 · Notify
Orderer is notified of the downgrade with the reason; the event feeds the monthly governance report. Downgrade never delays an already-checked-in patient's safety screening.
