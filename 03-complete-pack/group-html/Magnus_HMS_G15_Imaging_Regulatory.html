IR-01 Form F register

MVP1-CORE
G15 Imaging Regulatory — workspace 'Regulatory'
Baseline: EXTENDS · IR-S1 → Form F register
Purpose & workflow: The Form F register is the single source of truth for every PC-PNDT Form F generated at a centre — one record per prenatal ultrasound procedure, tamper-evident from the moment of capture. Compliance staff filter by month, resolve needs-attention items inline (missing signature, missing indication) before the monthly filing cutoff, and bulk-open records for review. Records feed IR-02 monthly submission prep; the filing month is determined by scan date, not entry date.

IR-04 Corrections & resubmission

MVP1-CORE
G15 Imaging Regulatory — workspace 'Regulatory'
Baseline: NET-NEW · C-2 corrections/resubmission
Purpose & workflow: Once a month is acknowledged its Form F records are immutable, so any error is remedied through a correction record: it links the original FF, classifies the error against a reason taxonomy, and queues the correction for inclusion in the next monthly submission. The system generates a correction letter for the Appropriate Authority and maintains a register of corrections with authority responses. Creating a correction requires step-up identity confirmation plus a second approver (four-eyes: Dr. A. Rao, Privacy Officer).
