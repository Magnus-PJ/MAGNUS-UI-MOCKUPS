GR-02 Log a Concern Wizard

MVP1-CORE
G14 + G11 — workspaces 'Governance' / 'Grievance'
Baseline: EXTENDS · GR-S2 wizard + anonymous channel
Purpose & workflow: Channel-agnostic intake for complaints: front desk logs on a patient's behalf, patients use their own link, or anyone can file anonymously (with a clear note on what anonymity limits). The category picker drives routing — privacy items auto-hand off to Governance, clinical concerns raise the care-safety flag — and the wizard nudges reporters not to paste unnecessary PHI. Every submission gets a GRV reference and a tracking explanation.

GR-04 Privacy handoff queue

MVP1-CORE
G11 Grievance & Support — workspace 'Grievance'
Baseline: NET-NEW · privacy handoff queue (GR↔GV bridge)
Purpose & workflow: Grievances classified as privacy/DPDP (consent disputes, unwanted messages, access complaints) auto-route here to the Privacy Officer with a combined SLA view — the grievance SLA and the DPDP 30-day statutory clock run in parallel and the tighter one drives colour. Disposition is one of: resolve as grievance, convert to a DPDP rights request (DPDP-CR), or convert to a security/privacy incident (INC). Cross-references are written both ways so neither trail goes dark.
