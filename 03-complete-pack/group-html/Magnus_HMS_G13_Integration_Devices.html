IN-03 Unknown AE & quarantine

MVP1-CORE
G13 Integration & Devices — workspace 'Integration'
Baseline: EXTENDS · RT-S4 unknown-AE → quarantine queue
Purpose & workflow: Studies pushed from AE titles not in the registry are quarantined instead of entering the archive — a privacy and integrity guard. Admins triage each quarantined study: register the sending AE (routes to Admin AD-08 with approval), associate it with a known device that was renamed, or reject with a reason. Study metadata stays masked until a decision is made; quarantined data auto-purges after 30 days.
