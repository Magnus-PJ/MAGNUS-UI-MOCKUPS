MVP1-CORE
G9 Billing & Payments — workspace 'Billing'
Baseline: EXTENDS · BL-S2 discounts → rules & limits
Purpose & workflow: Defines who may discount how much, and which named concession schemes exist (staff, senior citizen, camps). Cashiers apply discounts within their role cap at invoice time; anything above the cap auto-routes to the approvals queue (BL-08) with a mandatory reason. Every applied discount is attributable to a rule or scheme — no free-text "adjustments".
