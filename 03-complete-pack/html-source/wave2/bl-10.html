Purpose & workflow: The catalog is the single source of truth for billable services — code, SAC, modality, duration and base price — feeding the billing counter, appointment slots and GST documents. Prices change only through versioned price lists with effective dates (future-dated publish supported), branch overrides and payer/scheme columns. Every price change requires approval and lands in the change audit; nothing is edited in place on a live list.
