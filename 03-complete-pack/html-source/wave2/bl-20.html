MVP1-CORE

Baseline: NEW · expert-panel decision "Option C" — configurable release policy
Purpose & workflow: Implements the expert-panel decision Option C — organisation-configurable report release policy with authorised clinical override and full audit. The screen separates two statuses that the legacy system conflated: SIGNED (the clinical act is complete and the report is immutable — RD-09) and RELEASED (delivery of the report to patient/referrer is permitted — feeds NT-01 delivery). Policy is set once at organisation level (admin view, top); day-to-day the billing/front-office desk works the release queue (second frame). Reports flagged critical (RD-10) are never held for payment.

Report release policy ORG-LEVEL SETTING
Governs when a SIGNED report (RD-09) becomes RELEASED for delivery (NT-01). Effective-dated and recorded; applies to all branches of Meridian Diagnostics.
Release policy effective 01 Jul 2026
A — Release immediately on sign-off (payment independent)
Report is released to patient/referrer the moment the radiologist signs. Dues are pursued separately by the billing desk.
B — Withhold until full payment
Report stays SIGNED-not-released until balance = ₹0. No override path — not recommended; blocks clinically urgent delivery.
C — Withhold until payment, allow authorised clinical override SELECTED · PILOT DEFAULT
B behaviour day-to-day, but holders of release_without_payment may release on clinical grounds with reason + step-up confirm + audit. This is the panel-agreed pilot configuration ("B behaviour via C").
Change controlPolicy is org-level, recorded and effective-dated. Changing it requires Privacy Officer (S. Mehta) + org admin (K. Verma) joint approval; the change, both approvers and the effective date are written to the audit log. Last change: 01 Jul 2026 · B → C · CHG-POL-2026-031.
