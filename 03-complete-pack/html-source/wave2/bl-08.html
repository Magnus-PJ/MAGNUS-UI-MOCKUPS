Baseline: EXTENDS · BL-S2/S5 approvals → NS-06 async queue
Purpose & workflow: All money-sensitive requests — discounts over limit, refunds, invoice cancellations and write-offs — land in one async queue for the billing supervisor. The approver confirms identity before acting; rejections require a reason and bounce back to the requester with a notification. Requests untouched for 30 minutes past their hold window auto-void so counters are never blocked, and cashiers track everything in a my-requests view.
