Break-glass emergency access
For genuine clinical emergencies only. Every break-glass event is logged in full and reviewed by the branch privacy lead within 2 working days.
⚠ Use only when a patient's immediate care depends on it. Break-glass bypasses normal care-team access controls. Misuse is treated as a privacy incident under Meridian policy and the DPDP Act 2023 and may lead to suspension of Doctor Desk access.
Before you break the glass Checklist
✓I have a genuine, time-critical clinical reason.
✓The patient is not already on my care-team list (checked "My patients").
✓A standard access request (4h SLA) would be too slow for this situation.
✓I understand all my actions will be tagged and reviewed.
🔒 If this is not time-critical, use Access requests instead — the branch privacy lead approves most requests within 4 hours.
