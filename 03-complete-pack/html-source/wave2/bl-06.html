Baseline: EXTENDS · BL-S5 refunds + failure state
Purpose & workflow: Cashier raises a refund against a paid invoice/receipt — amount can never exceed the collected balance and a reason from the taxonomy is mandatory. Refunds route through the async approvals queue (BL-08); once approved they process on the original payment mode by preference, with a justified cash fallback. Failed bank/UPI refunds bounce into a recovery state with retry or alternate-method options, and every refund lands in the immutable refund register.
