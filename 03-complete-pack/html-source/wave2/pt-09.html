Purpose & workflow: Patients exercise their DPDP Act rights here without visiting the branch — request a copy of their records, a correction, deletion, or nominate someone to act for them. Identity is confirmed with an OTP to the registered mobile before anything is accepted, and every request gets a DPDP-CR tracking reference with a stated 30-day response window. Deletion requests explain plainly what the law requires us to keep, before the patient submits.

STATE C — DELETION REQUEST: WHAT WE CAN AND CANNOT DELETE YET
◎ Meridian Diagnostics · Andheri West branch
Before you ask us to delete PLEASE READ

We take deletion seriously and will remove everything we are allowed to. But some records must be kept for a fixed time under Indian law, even after you ask. In plain words:

WhatKept untilWhy (the law)
Scan reports & medical records3 years after your last visitMedical-records retention rules for diagnostic centres
Invoices & payment receipts8 years from the financial yearGST & income-tax record-keeping rules
Consent forms you signedAs long as the record they coverProof that we had your permission
X-ray / CT dose recordsPer AERB rulesRadiation-safety regulations

What we CAN do now: stop all reminders and marketing, remove your data from any research or analytics use, delete contact details not tied to a legal record, and lock the rest so it is only touched if the law demands it. When each retention period ends, the records are deleted automatically — you don't need to ask again.
