MVP1-CORE
G10 Notifications & Delivery — workspace 'Notifications'
Baseline: EXTENDS · NT-S1 delivery ledger
Purpose & workflow: The delivery ledger is the single source of truth for every outbound message tied to an accession — report-ready links, reminders, tokens and payment receipts. Staff filter by status, channel and date to answer "did the patient get it?" without opening the patient chart, and can drill into a per-accession timeline showing queued → sent → delivered → read/failed hops per recipient. Resends re-issue a fresh secure link (72-hour TTL, previous link revoked) and every view/resend/export is written to the access log.
