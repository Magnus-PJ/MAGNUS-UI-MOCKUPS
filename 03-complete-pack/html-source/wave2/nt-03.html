NT-03 Template admin

MVP1-CORE
G10 Notifications & Delivery — workspace 'Notifications'
Purpose & workflow: Admins manage one template per notification event (report-ready, reminder, token, payment), with a variant per language, each carrying its own TRAI DLT template ID. A lint panel blocks publishing on PHI leakage, unknown variables or segment-length issues, and any DLT registered-text mismatch hard-blocks sending on SMS. Every version change requires an approver and is kept in history with test-renders per variant.
