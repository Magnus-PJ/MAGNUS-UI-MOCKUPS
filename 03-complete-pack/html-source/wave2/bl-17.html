Purpose & workflow: Billing staff raise a write-off request against an aged outstanding balance; the request requires a documented collection-attempts log and a reason code. Only the org admin can approve, with step-up identity confirmation, since a write-off moves money off the books. Approved write-offs land in the branch write-off register with a monthly summary, and are automatically reversed if the patient later pays.
