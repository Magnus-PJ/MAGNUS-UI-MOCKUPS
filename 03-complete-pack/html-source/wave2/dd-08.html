Privacy & scope Policy

This page shows aggregates of your own referrals only. Magnus never shows referrers league tables, other doctors' figures, or incentive-linked metrics; referral analytics carry no payment linkage, consistent with Meridian's anti-inducement policy. CSV downloads contain the aggregate rows above and nothing else.

Trend view and CSV downloads (aggregates only, no identifiers) by Dr. Meera Nair on 25 Jul 2026 are recorded in the access log.

Referral analytics access is itself audited; any attempt to query another referrer's aggregates is denied and recorded in the access log.
