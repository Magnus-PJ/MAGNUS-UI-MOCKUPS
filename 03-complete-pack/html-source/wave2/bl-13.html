Place of supply rule explainer
CGST/SGST vs IGST — how Magnus decidesIf the recipient's place of supply is Maharashtra (state code 27, same as our GSTIN), a taxable line splits into CGST + SGST. If the recipient is registered in another state (e.g. a corporate payer with a Karnataka GSTIN), IGST applies. For walk-in patients, place of supply is the branch location — always Maharashtra, hence CGST/SGST.
