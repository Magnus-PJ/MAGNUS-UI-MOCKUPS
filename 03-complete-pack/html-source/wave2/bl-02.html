G9 Billing & Payments — workspace 'Billing'
Baseline: EXTENDS · BL-S2 invoice + C-5 GST fixes
Purpose & workflow: Cashier builds the invoice from the ordered services (rates and SAC codes pulled from the service catalog — no free-typed amounts), applies any discount which carries its own approval state, then issues the document. Issue is a legal, money-moving act: it pulls the next number from the central series registry and requires a step-up identity confirmation. Diagnostic services carry "Exempt (healthcare service)" labeling — never a 0% CGST/SGST row.
