NT-07 Quiet hours & rate rules

MVP1-CORE
G10 Notifications & Delivery — workspace 'Notifications'
Purpose & workflow: Central policy screen for when and how often Magnus may message patients. Non-urgent messages queue overnight (21:00–08:00 IST) and release in the morning; clinical alerts (critical results, STAT) are always exempt and never queued. Per-channel throttles and a per-patient daily cap protect patients and sender reputation, with a full audit of every exemption used.

⚠ CLINICAL ALERTS ARE EXEMPT — NEVER QUEUED. Critical-result notifications to referrers and STAT workflow alerts bypass quiet hours, throttles and daily caps at all times. This exemption cannot be disabled. Every exempt send is individually audited (see State C).
TRAI classification note. All Magnus patient messages are transactional/service communications (appointment, preparation, report-ready, billing) tied to a service the patient requested — none are promotional. Meridian sends no marketing from Magnus; DLT templates are registered in the service/transactional category. Quiet hours below are Meridian's own patient-courtesy policy, stricter than the regulatory minimum for transactional messages.
