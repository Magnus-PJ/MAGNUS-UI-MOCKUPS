Baseline: EXTENDS · IN-S3 + NT-S3 channels & senders
Purpose & workflow: Admins manage the delivery channels behind every patient/referrer message: SMS via DLT-registered entity and header, WhatsApp via a BSP with credential-expiry countdown and auto-alerts, and transactional email with SPF/DKIM domain health. The screen sets failover order, offers a sandbox mode that routes messages to test numbers only, and runs connection tests with a result log so provider issues are caught before patients notice.
