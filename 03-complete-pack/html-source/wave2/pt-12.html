I didn't get the OTP code ▴
✓
Wait 60 seconds — SMS can be slow at busy times. Then tap Resend code (allowed 3 times).
✓
Check the phone with the number ending in the digits shown on screen — the OTP goes to your registered mobile, which may not be the phone in your hand.
✓
If you use SMS-blocking / DND apps, check the blocked folder for sender MRDIAG.
✓
Still nothing after 3 tries? Call the clinic below — we can verify you by phone and re-send.
My registered mobile number is wrong or old ▾
For your safety we cannot change your number over the internet. Visit any Meridian branch with a photo ID (any one of passport, driving licence, voter ID, or Aadhaar — we record only the last 4 digits). The front office updates it in minutes and OTPs work immediately after.
The link says it has expired ▾
Report and reminder links stop working after a set time to protect you. Ask for a fresh link by calling the clinic, or collect a printed copy at the branch with photo ID. Expired links can never be revived — a new one is always issued instead.
My payment is stuck / money left my account ▾
If UPI money was debited but the page didn't confirm: don't pay again. Failed UPI payments auto-return within 2–5 working days by NPCI rules. Note your UPI/UTR reference from your bank app and call us — receipt RCP/AND/26-27/0xxx is issued the moment the money is confirmed on our side.
