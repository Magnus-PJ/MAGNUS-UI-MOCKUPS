Purpose & workflow: A plain-language page where any patient can see their DPDP rights — copy, correction, deletion, nominee, grievance — and start a request that lands in the privacy rights queue. It names the Privacy Officer and DPO, explains the 30-day path to the Data Protection Board if unresolved, and shows in simple words how long each kind of record is kept and why.
