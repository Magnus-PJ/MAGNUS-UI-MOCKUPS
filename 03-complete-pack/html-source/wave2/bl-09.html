Baseline: NET-NEW · NS-08 cash session & EOD close
Purpose & workflow: Each cashier opens a cash session with a counted float and closes it end-of-day through a fixed stepper: count → reconcile → resolve → sign. System totals by mode are compared against counted cash and gateway-settled digital amounts; any non-zero variance demands a reason and a supervisor countersign. The close produces an immutable CSH-AND-2026-xxxx record, and gateway mismatches link straight into the BL-04 reconciliation ledger.
