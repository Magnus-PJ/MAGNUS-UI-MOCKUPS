✓
Screen readers: these pages work with TalkBack (Android) and VoiceOver (iPhone). Buttons and OTP fields are labelled and announced.
✓
High contrast: if your phone is set to high-contrast or dark mode, the pages respect it. Colour is never the only signal — status is always written in words too.
✓
At the branch: if reading or signing is difficult, our front office will read forms aloud and a thumb impression with a witness is accepted where the law allows.
