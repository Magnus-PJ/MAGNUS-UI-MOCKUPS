Purpose & workflow: Authoritative register of litigation/regulatory holds. Placing a hold requires a scoped selection, legal reference and step-up identity confirmation; releasing one is irreversible in effect and therefore four-eyes gated (Privacy Officer + DPO). While a hold is active it suspends retention deletion, blocks DSR erasure with a statutory citation to the requester, and forces counsel exports through a logged channel. Review cadence keeps stale holds from lingering.

Effects while a hold is active enforced by platform, not by convention
Retention suspended Held records are excluded from expiry batches (GV-06) at compile time; no destruction job can include them.
DSR erasure refused Erasure requests over held records are refused with citation — "retention required for compliance with law / legal proceedings (DPDP §8(7), §12(3))" — and the refusal letter is stored on the request.
Counsel exports logged Export-for-counsel runs only through the disclosure register: named recipient, matter ref, watermark, hash manifest, access-logged.
