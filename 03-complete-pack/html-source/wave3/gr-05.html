GR-05 Patient tracking view

MVP1-CORE
G11 Grievance & Support — workspace 'Grievance'
Baseline: EXTENDS · GR-S3 → patient tracking page
Purpose & workflow: Public, patient-facing status page reached from the acknowledgement SMS: the patient enters the GRV reference and an OTP sent to their registered mobile. It shows an honest dated timeline (received → assigned → in progress → resolved), a plain-language "what we did" summary, an escalate option (branch manager first, Privacy Officer for privacy matters), and a reopen button that stays live for 30 days after resolution. No staff chrome, no clinical data — only the grievance itself.
