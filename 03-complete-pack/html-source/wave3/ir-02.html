Purpose & workflow: Prepares the centre's PC-PNDT Form F monthly filing: counts records by readiness, runs the full validation suite (patient signature, doctor PNDT validity, machine registration), and turns every failure into an owned fix-list item. The month cutoff is scan date — anything scanned in July files in the July batch regardless of when it was entered. A due-by-5th countdown drives escalation to the org admin and Privacy Officer if the batch risks going overdue.
