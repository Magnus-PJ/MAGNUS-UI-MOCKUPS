MVP1-CORE
G12 Admin — Org, People & Master Data — workspace 'Admin'
Baseline: EXTENDS · AD-S3 roles & permissions
Purpose & workflow: Admins review built-in and custom roles through a capability-group permission matrix (read / write / approve / export) with ABAC condition chips such as own-branch-only, care-team-only and masked-by-default. Any change to a role is drafted, versioned and held pending approval by a second admin before it takes effect. A simulate-user preview shows exactly what a role sees before granting it.
