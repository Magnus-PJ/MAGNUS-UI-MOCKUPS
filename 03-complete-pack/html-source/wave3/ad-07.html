Purpose & workflow: Front office and admins maintain the directory of external referring doctors: registration number, verified phone, report-delivery preferences and referrer-portal account state. New referrers arriving on paper referrals land in a pending queue until verified (registration lookup + proof); duplicates created at the counter are cleaned up with a merge tool that preserves referral history.
