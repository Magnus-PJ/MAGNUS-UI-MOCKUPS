GV-04 Deletion requests pipeline

MVP1-CORE
G14 Governance & Compliance — workspace 'Governance'
Purpose & workflow: DPDP erasure requests (DPDP-CR refs) move through received → identity-verified → assessed → approved → executed. Assessment splits data into delete-now, retention-held (radiology 10y, billing 8y, with keep-until dates) and legal-hold-blocked; execution is irreversible and therefore needs step-up identity plus a second approver. The patient is always sent a completion / partial-completion / refusal letter within the DPDP 30-day SLA; overdue requests escalate to the DPO.
