AD-06 Practitioner profiles

MVP1-CORE
G12 Admin — workspace 'Admin'
Baseline: EXTENDS · AD-S9 practitioners + C-6 mandatory registrations
Purpose & workflow: Org admins maintain practitioner profiles with the registrations that are mandatory per role — a radiologist cannot sign reports without a valid RMC registration, and a sonologist cannot perform obstetric US at a centre without a valid per-centre PNDT registration. Expiry countdowns (60/30/7-day chips) drive proactive renewal; on expiry the system auto-suspends the affected capability (sign-off, US acquisition) rather than the whole account.
