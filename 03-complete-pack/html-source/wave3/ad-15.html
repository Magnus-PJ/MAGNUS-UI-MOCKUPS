Purpose & workflow: Central registry of every printable document template — report header/footer, receipts, token slips, prep instructions, consent forms — with per-language variants, validated placeholders and approval-gated versioning. Editors preview with synthetic data only; a template cannot go live with unresolved placeholders, and each template lists exactly which document series render from it.
