MVP1-CORE
G12 Admin — Org, People & Master Data — workspace 'Admin'
Purpose & workflow: Admins configure departments per branch and the physical service points (desks, rooms) beneath them — each with a token prefix, queue link and modality mapping. Service points can be active or hidden (invisible to booking but preserved for history). Deactivation is guarded: a service point with an open queue cannot be switched off, and every change is written to the change audit.
