Baseline: EXTENDS · GV-S2 access log review
Purpose & workflow: Reviewers work a queue of automatically flagged PHI access events — after-hours access, non-care-team lookups, bulk reads and exports — plus a monthly 5% random sample. Each event gets a verdict: justified, needs explanation (creates a task for the user's manager), or violation (opens an incident). Violation verdicts require a second reviewer (four-eyes) before the incident is raised.
