Baseline: NET-NEW · audit export governance
Purpose & workflow: Every export of audit-log or patient-touching data leaves the system only through this controlled queue: requester states scope and justification, an approver runs a minimisation check (masking columns not needed for the stated purpose), and the file is issued as a watermarked, recipient-bound link with an expiry. A permanent export register records every issuance and supports one-click revoke; nothing is emailed as a raw attachment.
