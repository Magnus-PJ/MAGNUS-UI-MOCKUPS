CS-03 Procedure & contrast consent

MVP1-CORE
G5 Clinical Safety
Baseline: EXTENDS · CS-S2 consent + R-02 refusal/vernacular
Purpose & workflow: Technologists and front office capture informed consent for a procedure (and contrast, where applicable) immediately before the study, in the patient's chosen language. The screen must guarantee that consent text is rendered — not merely referenced — in the selected language, that risks, alternatives and the right to refuse are shown before signature, and that a refusal or pre-scan withdrawal hard-blocks the study, notifies the referrer, and preserves the record permanently. Guardians must state their relation; interpreters must attest.
