FO-22 Kiosk self check-in

CONDITIONAL
G3 Front Office & Reception — workspace 'Front Office'
Baseline: NET-NEW · kiosk self check-in (Conditional)
Purpose & workflow: Patients with an appointment check themselves in at a lobby kiosk using phone last-4 + OTP or their appointment reference, and receive a printed queue token. The screen must guarantee zero PHI beyond the patient's first name, large-touch accessible controls with a language toggle, and a hard gate: if any form, consent or payment is pending, the kiosk never states why on screen — it prints a desk slip and routes the patient to reception.
