What happens in Retract & rebind (Admin › Report governance)
Step 1
You raise a retraction request with the affected accession(s) and a mandatory narrative.
Step 2
Four-eyes approval: a second approver (branch admin K. Verma or branch medical lead Dr. A. Rao) must independently confirm — the requester can never self-approve.
Step 3
The report is retracted on both patient records with a visible retraction notice; every prior recipient is notified the report is withdrawn.
Step 4
The study is rebound to the correct patient/accession and returns to your reading worklist for a fresh report.
