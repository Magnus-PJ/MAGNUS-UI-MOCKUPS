Baseline: EXTENDS · RT-S2 hold/cancel → visible terminal states
Purpose & workflow: Technologists and supervisors use this screen to place studies on hold (with mandatory reason and auto-review timer) or cancel them against a fixed taxonomy. It must guarantee the radiation-delivered guard: once exposure has occurred a study can never be "cancelled" — it becomes the distinct terminal state abandoned after exposure, with the dose record retained forever. Terminal rows stay visible (struck through), and downstream parties (referrer, billing, reading) are notified automatically.
