Baseline: EXTENDS · FO-S3 Registration — consent step
Purpose & workflow: Final registration step: the patient (or guardian for minors) reviews the clinic privacy notice in their preferred language and grants purpose-level consent. Care and billing purposes are required to receive services; notifications and analytics are optional — analytics is unavailable for children. If a patient declines a required purpose, registration is not blocked: the refusal is recorded and flagged to the privacy team for follow-up. Staff-assisted (read-aloud) consent requires an attestation.
