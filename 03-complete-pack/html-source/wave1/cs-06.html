Baseline: EXTENDS · IR-S1 Form F + C-1 patient signature (NS-10)
Purpose & workflow: Before any obstetric ultrasound, the front office and performing doctor complete the statutory PC-PNDT Form F: centre and machine registration (validated for this centre), full patient particulars with address and ID (type + last-4 only), obstetric history G/P/L/A, an approved indication, the doctor's declaration and the patient's signed undertaking rendered in her language. The scan is hard-blocked until Form F is complete, the record is tamper-evident, and it feeds the monthly district submission. Foetal sex is never determined or disclosed.
