Baseline: NET-NEW · pre-sign quality checklist
Purpose & workflow: Runs automatically when the radiologist presses "Proceed to sign" and can be run on demand from the editor. Auto-checks compare findings vs impression, laterality vs order, template completeness, comparison-vs-linked-prior consistency and uncoded terms. Warnings never hard-block: the radiologist may acknowledge each and continue to sign, and every acknowledgement is attributed and logged. It also advises when an addendum to an existing signed report is more appropriate than a new report.
