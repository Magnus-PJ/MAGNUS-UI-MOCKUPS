Baseline: NET-NEW · adverse reaction record (clinical safety)
Purpose & workflow: Technologists and radiologists record any adverse reaction during or after a procedure — onset, severity, the exact agent and lot (pulled from TK-06 stock issue), interventions, outcome and reporter. The system must auto-alert the covering radiologist and branch medical lead, write a permanent allergy flag to the patient record with a propagation preview, and queue a pharmacovigilance export. Severe reactions surface an emergency checklist first, documentation second.
