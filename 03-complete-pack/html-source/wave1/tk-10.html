Baseline: EXTENDS · RT-S2 dose block → registry + DRL flags
Purpose & workflow: Technologists and the visiting medical physicist use this registry to review per-patient cumulative ionising dose (CTDIvol / DLP / DAP per study, 12-month rolling total) and per-unit dose trends against national DRLs. Studies exceeding DRL are auto-flagged as outliers and must carry a physicist note before the monthly close; the registry exports in AERB audit format. Dose values are captured from the modality at study completion and are never editable — only annotated.
