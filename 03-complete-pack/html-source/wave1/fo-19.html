G3 Front Office & Reception — workspace 'Front Office'
Purpose & workflow: Front office staff manage a per-modality waitlist for fully booked slots. When a booked appointment is cancelled, the system auto-offers the released slot to the highest-priority waitlisted patient whose time-window preference matches, via SMS with a hold expiry; staff can also make a manual offer. Patients see their queue position transparently; the system must guarantee first-match-first-offer ordering and that an expired offer automatically cascades to the next candidate.
