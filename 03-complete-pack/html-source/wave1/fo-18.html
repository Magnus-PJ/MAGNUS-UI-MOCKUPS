G3 Front Office & Reception — workspace 'Front Office'
Purpose & workflow: Front office scans or uploads paper referrals, prior reports and ID-adjacent documents (PDF/JPG/PNG) and attaches each to the correct patient + visit + order so nothing lives loose on a counter. Doc type comes from a fixed taxonomy which auto-assigns the retention class; referrer details are captured from the letterhead and matched against the referrer directory. OCR runs later — every scan carries an "OCR pending" placeholder flag until then, and files containing Aadhaar numbers are auto-flagged for redaction to last-4.
