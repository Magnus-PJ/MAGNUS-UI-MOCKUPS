TK-05 Retake / Repeat Exposure

MVP1-CORE
G6 Radiology Technician — workspace 'RIS · Technician'
Purpose & workflow: The technologist raises a retake when acquired images are non-diagnostic (motion, artefact, positioning, equipment). For ionising modalities (X-ray/CT) the system must capture a radiation justification with an additional dose estimate and route it to an authorising radiologist before re-exposure. Once authorised, the expected new series is registered so the image-receipt table (TK-04) knows to expect it, and every retake feeds the branch QA dashboard with reason taxonomy for repeat-rate analysis.
