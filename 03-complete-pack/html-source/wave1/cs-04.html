CS-04 Physician override — blocked screening

MVP1-CORE
G5 Clinical Safety
Baseline: NET-NEW · closes S-2 physician-only override (was CS-S1 self-override)
Purpose & workflow: When a safety screening (contrast, MRI, pregnancy) blocks a study, the technologist or desk can only request an override — they can never grant one. The request lands in a physician queue; the physician reviews the underlying screening data, must confirm their identity, give a clinical reason, and acknowledge the risk statement. A grant is time-boxed and stamped onto the study as a banner; every step is fully audited.
