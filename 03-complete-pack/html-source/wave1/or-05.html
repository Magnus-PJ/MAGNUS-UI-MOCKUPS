Purpose & workflow: Controlled change surface for an existing order, used by front office and branch admins. It must guarantee that what can change depends strictly on order status (indication editable until acquired; a procedure change is always cancel + new linked order, never an in-place edit), that cancellation states its reason from a fixed taxonomy and lists every downstream effect, and that post-acquisition cancellation is guarded because radiation has already been delivered. Every change produces a new order version.
