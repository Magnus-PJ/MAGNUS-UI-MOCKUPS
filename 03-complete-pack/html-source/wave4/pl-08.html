Purpose & workflow: Operators promote Magnus platform versions through rings (sandbox → pilot → GA) with a mandatory checklist, and manage edge-agent versions per branch. Rollbacks pass through a data-migration guard: if a migration is not reversible, rollback is blocked and a forward-fix is required. Edge upgrades roll out in waves, with offline branches automatically deferred until they reconnect.
