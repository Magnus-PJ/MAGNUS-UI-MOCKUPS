AU-09 Step-up identity confirmation

MVP1-CORE
G1 Identity & Access — auth entry + session
Baseline: EXTENDS · AU-S6 step-up + action matrix
Purpose & workflow: Sensitive actions (signing a report, approving a refund at/above limit, merging patient records, closing an org) interrupt the flow with a standard step-up modal that names the exact action and the mechanism required by org policy (passkey, TOTP, or password). The confirmation is bound to that single action, expires in 5 minutes, and every attempt — pass or fail — writes a structured audit line.

Implementation noteStep-up issues a short-lived confirmation token bound to the session AND the specific target object (e.g. the report being signed) — prevents CSRF/replay.
Audit line format structured, append-only
stepup.<pass|fail|expired> · actor=<user> · action=<verb.object> · target=<id> · mech=<passkey|totp|password> · attempt=n/N · <ts IST> · term=<terminal>
Confirmations are single-use and bound to the exact action payload shown in the modal — a confirmation cannot be replayed for a different target. Expired confirmations (5 min) require a fresh step-up.

Viewing the matrix is logged read-only; any edit to a matrix row requires step-up (passkey) plus counter-approval by a second admin, both recorded in the access log.
