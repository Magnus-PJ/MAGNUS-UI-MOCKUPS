Purpose & workflow: Operators hold zero standing access to tenant clinical data. In an emergency (data-loss recovery, court order, critical incident) an operator requests break-glass access with a reason category; a second operator must approve before any access is granted. Sessions are time-boxed to 4 hours, the tenant is notified immediately, every action is recorded, and an evidence bundle plus mandatory post-review closes each event.
