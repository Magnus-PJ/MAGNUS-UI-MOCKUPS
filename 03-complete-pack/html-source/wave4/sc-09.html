Lane rules
✓Separate lanes for walk-in vs booked — booked patients hold their slot time; walk-ins queue FIFO in their own lane. Interleave ratio:
✓Urgent-first — STAT/urgent tokens (flagged by technologist or radiologist) jump both lanes; the deferred patient keeps position #2. Toggle per modality: CT on · US on · MRI on · X-ray off
✓No-show recycling — 3 unanswered calls over 10 min moves token to end of lane; 2 recycles then marks no-show.
 Priority lane for senior citizens / accessibility (adds a third lane) — off in draft v12.
