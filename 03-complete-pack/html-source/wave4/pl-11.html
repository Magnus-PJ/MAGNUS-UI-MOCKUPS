Meter definitions Billing glossary
Study
One DICOM study registered and stored, counted at first C-STORE acceptance. Rejected/QC-discarded studies are excluded within 72h.
Storage GB
Peak stored volume during the month (hot + nearline PACS tiers), sampled daily at 02:00 IST.
SMS / WhatsApp
Provider-confirmed deliveries only (DLT Jio route, WhatsApp BSP). Failed/undelivered messages are not billed.
API call
Authenticated external API requests (HL7/FHIR + partner API). Internal service traffic is never metered.
Seat
Distinct staff account active in the billing month (≥1 sign-in). Patient OTP identities are never seats.
Tenant-visible mirror
Same numbers, both sidesEach tenant admin sees this exact meter under Settings › Subscription & usage in their own console. Mirrors update from the same metering pipeline within 15 minutes — operators cannot show a tenant different figures.
