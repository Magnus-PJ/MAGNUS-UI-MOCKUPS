PL-06 Support access

MVP1-CORE
G16 Platform Operator — operator console
Baseline: EXTENDS · PL-S5/S7 support access + revoke
Purpose & workflow: Operators never have standing access to tenant data. To assist with a ticket, an operator requests a tenant-approved support session with explicit scope and duration; the tenant admin approves, a banner appears in the tenant UI for the whole session, and every action is tagged. The tenant (or the operator) can revoke mid-session with immediate effect, and an evidence bundle of all actions is auto-shared to the tenant when the session ends.
