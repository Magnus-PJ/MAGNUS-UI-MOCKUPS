SC-02 Holidays & closures

MVP1-CORE
G4 Scheduling & Resource Setup — workspace 'Setup'
Baseline: EXTENDS · FO-S8 holidays & closures
Purpose & workflow: The org admin maintains the closure calendar per branch: public holidays that repeat yearly and one-off closures, either whole-branch or limited to a modality. Before any closure is saved the system previews existing bookings on that date; if there are conflicts, saving spawns a bulk-reschedule task so no patient is silently dropped. Closures override working hours (SC-01) in slot generation.
