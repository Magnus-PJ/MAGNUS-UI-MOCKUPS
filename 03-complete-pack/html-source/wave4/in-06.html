MVP1-CORE
G13 Integration & Devices — workspace 'Integration'
Baseline: NET-NEW · retry/replay console (scope item #103)
Purpose & workflow: Failed HL7/DICOM/comms messages land in per-connector queues where staff can replay a single message or a filtered batch. Replays are idempotency-safe: every message carries a stable key, so a duplicate replay is acknowledged but suppressed downstream. Messages that fail repeatedly move to the POISON queue, which auto-raises an engineer task and suppresses further automatic retries.
