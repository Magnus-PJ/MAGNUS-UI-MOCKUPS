Baseline: EXTENDS · FO-S8 slot templates
Purpose & workflow: Slot templates define how bookable time is cut per modality: slot length, patients per slot, prep gap between slots, and whether walk-ins or overbooking are allowed. Each template is versioned; the calendars that consume it are listed so an admin can see the blast radius before editing. Templates that are in use by future generated slots cannot be deleted — they are retired with an effective date instead.
