SC-06 Modality calendar admin & publishing

MVP1-CORE
G4 Scheduling setup — workspace 'Setup'
Baseline: NET-NEW · calendar publish/versioning
Purpose & workflow: Admin generates the bookable slot calendar per modality from working hours + slot templates + device availability, previews the diff against the currently published version, then publishes with an effective date and version tag. Booked slots are never silently dropped — a regenerate that would remove a slot with an active booking is blocked until the booking is moved. Rollback restores a prior version under the same guard, with dual-control sign-off.
