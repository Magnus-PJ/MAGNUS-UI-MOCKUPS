Purpose & workflow: Single status board for every external connector the branch depends on — PACS/archive, modality worklist (MWL/MPPS), messaging providers (DLT SMS, WhatsApp BSP, email), payment gateway (UPI) and edge-sync. Org admins and platform ops triage from here: each tile links to the connector detail, the incident queue and the 24-hour error-rate table. Auto-alert rules escalate to the on-call channel when a connector breaches its threshold; no patient data appears on this screen.
