MVP1-CORE
G1 Identity & Access — profile + degraded
Baseline: NET-NEW · P2 admin unlock & recovery approvals
Purpose & workflow: Org/branch admins work a single queue of account lockouts, lost-authenticator recovery requests and suspicious-sign-in holds, each with an SLA and owner. Before approving, the admin must complete an identity-verification checklist (callback on the known number, manager confirmation); approval itself requires step-up, and denials require a reason. Every action lands in a tamper-evident register.
