AU-02 Sign-in failures & throttling

MVP1-CORE
G1 IDENTITY & ACCESS — AUTH ENTRY
Baseline: EXTENDS · AU-S1/S4 failure & lockout states
Purpose & workflow: Defines every failure path off AU-01: wrong password with attempts-remaining counter, progressive throttling with a visible countdown, hard lock after the policy threshold, and a distinct suspended-account message. Copy is written to avoid account enumeration — failure text never confirms whether an email exists, and suspension is only disclosed after a correct password.
