Purpose & workflow: Staff who forget their password request a reset from the sign-in page. The system always shows the same neutral confirmation (no account enumeration), then delivers a one-time link by email plus a 6-digit OTP that must be entered together. The new password must satisfy org policy (min 12 chars, last-5 history block, no forced periodic rotation); on success all other sessions are revoked.
